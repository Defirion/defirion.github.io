Playlist mixer

I host salsa and bachata parties, and I wanted the music split by time on the dance floor. The mixer gives each genre a share of the listening time, with songs in runs: bachata in pairs, salsa in ones or twos.

This autumn I corrected and tested the mixing logic and rebuilt the interface as a hardware-style console.

Simulated tracks between 3:00 and 4:30. Preset and code in the public repository. There is a live app, but Spotify only lets accounts on its allowlist sign in.

WoW addons and companion

A set of World of Warcraft addons and a companion app for inventory, market analysis, crafting and warehousing. The companion scans auction data across 81 EU realms and ranks them. Changes to that ranking are tested on realms held out of the fit, and most have been rejected.

A sister set does the same job for Forever. There every item is followed to its sale, so projected gold per hour can be checked against what it really earned. A graph will be added when it launches.

I define the operating logic: which tool owns each action, and when incomplete data should stop an automated step.

Tabletop adaptations

Digital versions of tabletop games we own, played at home over the local network. Everyone joins from their phone by QR code; a TV can show the board but isn't needed.

There are two games so far. Both run through Tabletop Nexus, a portal I built that starts one game at a time behind a single address. The portal is public; the games stay private because the rules and art aren't mine to share.

After family playtests I reworked the animations and icons so moves were easier to follow.

Homelab

A small Proxmox host. OPNsense is the firewall and router, so everything from outside passes through it, and AdGuard filters DNS. A Debian VM runs Docker for my own services, like a panel that runs the bedroom AC on a schedule.

I'm reworking it, on paper first, with an inventory, a network map and a change log: separate network segments, alerting, intrusion detection, and a message bus the services share.

Dashed boxes are planned. Hover a box for what it does.
